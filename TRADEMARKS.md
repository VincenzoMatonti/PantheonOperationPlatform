# Pantheon Trademarks

## Overview

Pantheon is an open source project developed and maintained by **Vincenzo Matonti**.

The Pantheon source code is licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)**.

The software license governs the use, modification, and distribution of the source code. It does not grant trademark rights to the **Pantheon** name, logo, or other project branding.

## Project Name and Branding

The name **Pantheon** identifies the official project, its software, and its associated ecosystem.

The Pantheon name and project branding should be used in a way that does not create confusion about:

* the origin of a product or service;
* whether a project is officially maintained by Pantheon;
* whether a company is affiliated with Pantheon;
* whether a product or service is officially supported or endorsed by Pantheon.

## Forks and Modified Versions

The GPL-3.0 license permits users and organizations to:

* use Pantheon;
* modify Pantheon;
* redistribute Pantheon;
* build products and services based on Pantheon.

Forks and modified versions should clearly distinguish themselves from the official Pantheon project when necessary.

Use of the Pantheon name must not imply that a fork, distribution, integration, or modified version is an official Pantheon release unless explicitly authorized.

## Commercial Use

Commercial use of Pantheon software is permitted under the GPL-3.0 license.

Organizations and individuals may build commercial products and services around Pantheon, including:

* integrations;
* extensions;
* consulting services;
* hosting services;
* managed deployments;
* enterprise solutions;
* support services;
* other commercial offerings.

Commercial use of the software does not by itself imply affiliation with or endorsement by the official Pantheon project.

## Official Pantheon Services

The Pantheon project may provide official services and offerings, including:

* Official support
* Official integrations
* Managed hosting
* Cloud services
* Consulting
* Enterprise services

Only services explicitly identified as official should be represented as official Pantheon services.

## Use of the Pantheon Logo

The Pantheon logo and other official visual identities are intended to identify the official project.

Use of project logos or other branding should not suggest official affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement where none exists.

## Future Trademark Policy

This document represents the initial trademark policy for Pantheon.

As the project and its ecosystem grow, additional guidelines may be introduced for:

* commercial use of the Pantheon name;
* use of project logos;
* community distributions;

Questions regarding the use of Pantheon branding should be raised through the project's official communication channels.
